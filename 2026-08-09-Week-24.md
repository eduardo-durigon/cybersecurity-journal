## Topics Studied

### Final Core 1 Revision

The days leading up to the exam were focused on identifying and eliminating as many remaining weak areas as possible.

My final revision included:

* Completing practice exams
* Reviewing incorrect and uncertain answers
* Revisiting weaker topics
* Memorising common port numbers and protocols
* Reinforcing Wi-Fi standards and frequency ranges
* Reviewing troubleshooting procedures
* Filling any remaining gaps across the Core 1 objectives

At this stage, preparation became increasingly focused on the smaller details that are easy to forget under exam pressure. Memorising technical information was important, but I also continued focusing on understanding how that knowledge could be applied in troubleshooting scenarios.

---

### Exam Preparation and Strategy

I also made a conscious effort to manage the final hours before the exam properly.

The night before, I prioritised getting a good night's sleep rather than attempting a last-minute study marathon. On exam day, I kept revision very light and avoided overwhelming myself with additional information.

During the exam, I used the available time carefully.

My approach included:

* Reading every question carefully
* Using the full exam time available
* Reviewing every answer before submitting
* Flagging questions I was uncertain about
* Reconsidering answers when necessary
* Taking calculated risks on questions where I was unsure

Having enough time for a complete review was particularly valuable. Rather than rushing to finish, I was able to revisit difficult questions and make final decisions based on everything I had learned throughout my preparation.

---

### Performance-Based Questions

One of the biggest surprises was the performance-based section of the exam.

I received **seven performance-based questions**, and they were all different from the practice material I had encountered while studying with the official CompTIA resources.

This ended up reinforcing one of the most important lessons from my preparation: **over-preparing was worth it**.

Instead of relying on recognising familiar practice questions, I had to understand the underlying concepts well enough to apply them to unfamiliar scenarios. The practical labs, repeated revision, troubleshooting exercises, and additional preparation made a significant difference when facing questions I had never seen before.

It was a good reminder that certification preparation should be about developing real understanding rather than memorising specific practice questions.

---

## Key Takeaways

The biggest takeaway from this week is that preparation creates confidence.

Walking into the exam knowing that I had done everything reasonably possible to prepare allowed me to focus on solving the problems in front of me rather than worrying about what I might have missed.

The performance-based questions were a perfect example. They were unfamiliar, but the concepts behind them were not.

Another important lesson was the value of managing the exam itself. Getting enough sleep, keeping the final day light, using the entire available exam time, and reviewing every answer were all small decisions that contributed to the final result.

After weeks of studying, labs, quizzes, practice exams, revision, and troubleshooting exercises, seeing the final score of **784/900** was an incredibly rewarding moment.

**CompTIA A+ Core 1: passed.**
